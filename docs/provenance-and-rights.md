# Provenance and rights

AI Trajectory publishes structured claims about third-party forecasts and public measurements. The
project's original code, data model, assessments, and documentation have explicit licenses; source
material does not become relicensed merely because it appears here.

## Source classes

- `data/scoreboard/claims.json` contains short attributed excerpts or faithful claim statements,
  source URLs, conditionality, and original project assessments.
- `data/scoreboard/metrics.json` contains project-compiled observations and links to the underlying
  datasets, filings, announcements, or reporting.
- `docs/harvest/` preserves raw AI-assisted research outputs, including source excerpts and
  secondary-source notes. It is an unverified research archive, not a replacement for the originals.
- Generated pages in `dashboard/` are build artifacts and are not tracked in Git.

## Quotation policy

Use only the amount of source text needed to identify and assess a claim. Attribute it, link the
original, preserve material qualifications, and prefer an accessible primary source. Do not copy
paywalled article bodies or redistribute third-party datasets. When a primary source cannot be
checked, label the item as secondary or unverified and exclude it from headline results.

Third-party quotations and source material remain subject to their original copyright, license, and
terms. They are excluded from `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`. This repository does not claim ownership of
source titles, names, or trademarks.

## Verification state

The legacy crux corpus received a citation-by-citation audit. The raw harvest archive did not. The
current structured dataset distinguishes direct sources, compiled points, provenance gaps,
self-reporting, and proxy relationships. See [`research-integrity.md`](research-integrity.md) for the
scope of past verification and the correction policy.
